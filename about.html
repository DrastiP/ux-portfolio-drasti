About

Design as structure, not decoration.

Hi, I'm Drasti, a product designer with a background in Architectural Engineering from the University of Waterloo. I was drawn to design through its ability to shape how people move through both physical and digital environments, and how thoughtful structure can influence everyday decisions.

At Arctic Wolf, I worked on incident response experiences, designing tools that supported teams operating in high pressure, complex situations. That experience reinforced my focus on consistency and intentionality, and the importance of creating systems that feel clear, reliable, and easy to navigate when it matters most.

I approach design through systems and structure, focusing on how parts connect into cohesive experiences. Feel free to reach out or view my resume.
