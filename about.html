About

Bringing structure to complex systems.

Hi, I'm Drasti, a Product Designer with a background in Architectural Engineering from the University of Waterloo. My work focuses on complex software, where product design means making sense of systems, workflows, information, and constraints — and turning them into experiences that support real work.

At Arctic Wolf, I designed enterprise security products used by teams investigating incidents and making high-stakes decisions with large volumes of information. I worked across research, product definition, interaction design, and delivery, partnering closely with product and engineering to take complex problems from ambiguity through launch.

My approach is grounded in systems thinking: understanding how individual workflows fit together, how information should be structured, and how product decisions create a coherent experience across a larger system.
